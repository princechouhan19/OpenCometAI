# v1.15.2: Indian ID expansion and the live per-task scorecard

A field screenshot of a government scheme form showed the Aadhaar box correctly
black-barred while a partially typed PAN ("ABCDE1234", missing the check letter)
stayed visible. The strict 10-character PAN pattern could not match it, and the
DOM hint list only knew `pan[-_]?number`, so the field itself was never
redacted.

## Detector expansion (`pii-detector.js`)

- Partial and mid-entry PAN, context-gated on a PAN label within ±40 characters.
- Voter ID and EPIC (`ABC1234567`).
- Indian passport, context-gated.
- Driving licence: state, RTO, year and serial, context-gated.
- IFSC, and UPI VPA matched on known PSP and bank handles, so email addresses
  never match.
- GSTIN, which wins the span from the embedded PAN in deduplication.
- Bank account numbers, label-gated and digits-only, so the label never leaks
  through the mask.
- CVV and card PIN assignments, masked as secret spans.
- 16-digit Aadhaar Virtual IDs, with Verhoeff extended to 16 digits.

A labelled driving licence also wins its span from the greedy phone detector.

## DOM layer

The sensitive-input hint list now covers every Indian ID family, so a field
named `pan`, `voter`, `passport`, `dl`, `ifsc`, `upi` or `gstin` is black-barred
whole, the same treatment the Aadhaar box already received. All new types
render solid black.

## SIH scorecard

In Settings, Privacy & Vision, the panel now explains what it is for.
Benchmark rows fill by importing reports. Live rows (VLM p50, sanitize p50 and a
per-task privacy report) fill automatically when a privacy task completes, from
the DONE summary's measured census: redactions by family, frames, payload and
verification. They persist across panel reloads. Untouched rows still read a
dash rather than a fabricated number.
