# v1.15.3: small profile photos

A field report showed every PII family redacted correctly
(`{"password":1,"sensitive_input":9,"phone":2,"pan":1,"dob":1,"email":2}`) while
a small profile photo stayed visible with "Faces detected: 0". The user had
noticed repeatedly that small profile images escape the blur.

## Root cause

The pixel cascade (full frame, 512 px tiles, 256 px fine tiles, then YOLO
person crops) is probabilistic. Tile caps sample positions, a head-only crop
inside a small `<img>` can sit below every stage's threshold, and YOLO rarely
boxes a thumbnail person, so the person-guided sweep had no region to work
with.

## Fix: a deterministic DOM-guided face sweep

`pageContextScan` now also returns `photoCandidates`: the rects of every
visible, decoded `<img>` of 24 px or larger. Avatar-likeness hints, roundness
and small size only sort the list; nothing is excluded. The list is capped at
32 regions and carries rects only, no image data.

The pipeline crops each region not already covered by a detected face,
upscales it to a 256 px minimum edge with a ×4 cap (a 64 px avatar face becomes
about 200 px), and lets the same MediaPipe detector confirm it, at confidence
0.30, `source: mediapipe-dom-crop`, about 5 to 85 ms per region.

Only model-confirmed faces are redacted, so logos and generic icons are scanned
but never blurred. When the cascade already caught the face, the sweep stands
down and costs nothing. The console reports the stage as
`Faces(dom-sweep): N detected …`.

## Verification

New `scripts/test_dom_face_sweep.mjs` 17/17 on real browser pixels with the
real vendored model: collector behaviour (skips under 24 px and src-less
images, caps at 32), rescue with the cascade disabled (0 faces, then the sweep
finds one and the box lands on the avatar), stand-down when the cascade covers
it, no redaction of icons, and DPR 1.25 coordinate mapping.
